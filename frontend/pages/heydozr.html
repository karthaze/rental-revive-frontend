How it answers

Two tracks, and the machine decides which, not the customer.

A contractor should never have to work out whether his job is the simple kind. He describes it, and the equipment itself settles what happens next.

Instant Reserve

For machines a number can describe.

Dig depth, working height, transport width, operating weight. Those are figures off a spec sheet, not judgment calls. He says he needs three metres of depth behind a building with a 2.4 metre gate; the system matches it, checks what is actually free on those dates, and holds the unit. At ten at night, without waiting on anybody.

Quote Required

For anything that comes off a load chart.

Crane and rigging capacity changes with radius, so it is never priced automatically. Not by us, not by software, not ever. The job gets captured properly, weight, pick height, set height, radius, access, ground conditions, and lands with your estimator as a complete brief. He prices it. He just stops having to ring the customer back to find out what the job is.

That second paragraph is the part worth reading twice. Every owner in this business has been pitched software that claims it can quote a lift. We are telling you plainly that we refuse to, and why.
